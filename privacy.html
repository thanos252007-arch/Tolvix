Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Overview

Tolvix is a collection of online utilities. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate, understand and protect the website.

Tool inputs

Many tools process text and numbers locally in your browser. Tolvix does not intentionally send those inputs to its own server when you use those tools. Check the individual tool page for any feature-specific notice.

QR Code Generator

The QR tool uses the external service at api.qrserver.com. The text or URL you enter is sent to that service to create the QR image. The provider may process it under its own privacy practices. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into this feature.

AI Detector & Humanizer

This tool uses local, rule-based text analysis and editing in your browser. Text entered into this tool is not sent to Tolvix's server or to an AI API by this tool. Its style score is a heuristic indicator, not an AI probability or proof of authorship. Review suggested edits because they may affect meaning or nuance.

Hosting and technical data

Tolvix is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

Analytics and any future advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies. The specific services and choices are described in the sections below. Tolvix does not currently ask visitors to create an account or submit information through a site-owned contact form.

Browser processing libraries

Some PDF tools load pdf-lib and PDF.js from public content delivery networks, and the JavaScript minifier loads Terser from a CDN. These libraries run in your browser to process the files or code you provide; Tolvix does not intentionally upload those inputs to its own file-processing server. The CDN providers may receive ordinary network request information when the library files are downloaded.

External links

We do not control third-party websites or services. Review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Information collected through website visits

When you open a page, the hosting and security infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps and security events. This information helps deliver the website, detect abuse and troubleshoot service problems. The handling and retention of infrastructure logs are subject to the relevant provider's terms and privacy documentation.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics measurement ID G-WH86KPZW58 to understand aggregate website activity, such as page views, traffic sources, device categories and interactions. Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on browser settings and the configuration in effect. Google processes analytics information under its own terms and policies. You can review Google's information at Google's Privacy Policy and learn about available controls in your browser or Google account.

Advertising and advertising cookies

Tolvix may use Google AdSense or another advertising service in the future. If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and limit ads. Depending on the user's settings, location and the consent choices presented, ads may be personalised based on previous visits to this or other websites, or may be non-personalised. Advertising partners may process device, browser, approximate location, ad interaction and other information as described in their own privacy policies.

When ads are introduced, this policy and the site's consent controls will be updated to describe the services actually used and the choices available. Users can review Google's advertising controls at Google Ads Settings and Google's explanation of how Google uses information for advertising. Browser settings can also restrict or clear cookies, although some site features may be affected. The availability of an opt-out depends on the provider, region and applicable rules.

Third-party services and browser libraries

The QR Code Generator sends the text or URL entered into it to api.qrserver.com to create a QR image. Some PDF tools load pdf-lib or PDF.js from public content delivery networks, and the JavaScript minifier loads Terser from a CDN. These libraries run in your browser to process the files or code you provide; Tolvix does not intentionally upload those inputs to its own file-processing server. The providers may receive ordinary network request information when their services or library files are accessed. Review the individual tool page before using it with confidential information.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use a tool that relies on an external service, adjust browser cookie controls, or use available provider-level privacy settings. Blocking some scripts may affect analytics, advertising or tool functionality. For privacy questions or requests relating to Tolvix, use the Contact page and avoid posting personal or confidential information in a public issue.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy when tools, analytics, hosting or advertising arrangements change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.
