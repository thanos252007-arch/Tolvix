Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Overview

Tolvix is a collection of online utilities. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate, understand and protect the website.

Tool inputs

Many tools process text and numbers locally in your browser. Tolvix does not intentionally send those inputs to its own server when you use those tools. Check the individual tool page for any feature-specific notice.

QR Code Generator

The QR tool uses the external service at api.qrserver.com. The text or URL you enter is sent to that service to create the QR image. The provider may process it under its own privacy practices. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into this feature.

AI Detector & Humanizer

This tool uses local, rule-based text analysis and editing in your browser. Text entered into this tool is not sent to Tolvix's server or to an AI API by this tool. Its style score is a heuristic indicator, not an AI probability or proof of authorship. Review suggested edits because they may affect meaning or nuance.

Hosting and technical data

Tolvix is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

Tolvix uses Google Analytics to understand website usage. Google may collect information such as page views, device and browser details and interaction data under its own policies. If advertising, accounts or forms are introduced, this policy must be reviewed and updated to describe the data practices and choices that apply. Third-party services may use their own technologies under their respective policies.

Browser processing libraries

Some PDF tools load pdf-lib and PDF.js from public content delivery networks, and the JavaScript minifier loads Terser from a CDN. These libraries run in your browser to process the files or code you provide; Tolvix does not intentionally upload those inputs to its own file-processing server. The CDN providers may receive ordinary network request information when the library files are downloaded.

External links

We do not control third-party websites or services. Review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Tolvix changes. For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
