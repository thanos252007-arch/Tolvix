Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Overview

Tolvix is a collection of online utilities. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate, understand and protect the website.

Tool inputs

Many tools process text, numbers and selected files locally in your browser. Tolvix does not intentionally send those inputs to its own file-processing server when you use those tools. This includes the UTM Link Builder, Reading Time Calculator, Meta Tag Generator, Regex Tester, Markdown Table Generator and robots.txt Generator. Check each tool page for feature-specific details. Analytics may still record page usage and interaction events.

QR Code Generator

The QR tool uses the external service at api.qrserver.com. The text or URL you enter is sent to that service to create the QR image. The provider may process it under its own privacy practices. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into this feature.

Hosting and technical data

Tolvix is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Analytics, cookies and local storage

Google Analytics is active on Tolvix (measurement ID G-WH86KPZW58) to measure website activity, including page views, traffic sources, device categories and interactions. The Google Analytics tag is loaded on site pages and may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on Google's configuration and your browser settings. Google processes analytics information under its own terms and policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Current analytics consent controls: As of 21 November 2026, Tolvix does not display a site-wide cookie banner or an on-page control for accepting, rejecting or changing analytics-cookie preferences. The current page implementation loads and configures the Google Analytics tag directly; the source code reviewed does not show a Consent Mode default or a consent choice being passed to Google. Visitors can block or clear cookies through browser settings, use privacy extensions, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on where available. These browser-level options are not an on-site consent control. Where applicable law requires prior consent for analytics cookies or similar storage, Tolvix must implement and test an appropriate consent mechanism and configure its analytics tag accordingly before relying on analytics for visitors in those locations.

Tolvix also stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage under the key tolvix-theme. This is a site preference used to remember your selected appearance. Tolvix does not currently require an account or provide a site-owned account profile. Contact options are described on the Contact page.

Third-party services used by tools

Some tools contact third-party providers or download code and model files from content delivery networks. Those providers may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser/device details, requested resource and request time. The following describes the external services identified in the current tool source code:

  • QR Code Generator — QRServer: The text or URL you enter is included in a request to api.qrserver.com to generate the QR image. This means the QR content is sent to that provider. Do not submit confidential information.
  • Currency Converter — ExchangeRate-API: The browser requests a public USD-based exchange-rate table from open.er-api.com. The amount you enter and the selected currencies are used in the browser calculation and are not included in that rate-table request.
  • PDF tools — jsDelivr, cdnjs and/or unpkg: PDF features download libraries such as pdf-lib and PDF.js from these CDNs. The PDF files selected for processing are read and processed in your browser by the tool; the inspected implementation does not send those PDF files to these CDNs or to a Tolvix file-processing server.
  • JavaScript Minifier — jsDelivr: The Terser library is downloaded from jsDelivr. The JavaScript text you enter is processed in the browser and is not sent to jsDelivr by the minifier.
  • AI Background Remover — jsDelivr and Hugging Face: The browser downloads the Transformers.js runtime and background-removal model files. The inspected implementation performs image segmentation in the browser; it does not upload the selected image to Hugging Face for processing. The providers may receive technical information when runtime or model files are requested.
  • AI Text-to-Speech — jsDelivr and cdnjs: The browser downloads the Kokoro runtime/model and MP3 encoder library. The inspected implementation generates speech in the browser; it does not send the entered text to those library CDNs for speech generation. The providers may receive technical information when library or model files are requested.

Other tools such as image conversion, image resizing, text utilities, physics calculations and chemistry calculations are implemented to process their inputs in the browser based on the source code reviewed. Tool implementations can change, so check the relevant tool page and this policy for updates. The Word to PDF utility uses your browser's built-in print dialog; Tolvix does not upload the text to generate the print view.

External links

We do not control third-party websites or services. Review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Information collected through website visits

When you open a page, the hosting and security infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps and security events. This information helps deliver the website, detect abuse and troubleshoot service problems. The handling and retention of infrastructure logs are subject to the relevant provider's terms and privacy documentation.

Advertising and advertising cookies

Current status (21 November 2026): Google AdSense and other display-advertising tags are not currently enabled on Tolvix. Tolvix does not currently serve display ads or intentionally set advertising cookies for ad delivery. Google Analytics is active as described above; its analytics cookies or identifiers are separate from advertising cookies.

If Tolvix enables Google AdSense or another advertising service, Google and its participating advertising vendors may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies. These technologies may be used to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, detect invalid activity and, where permitted, personalise advertising. Vendors may use information from a visitor's visits to Tolvix and other websites or apps, subject to the visitor's location, applicable law, consent choices and available settings. Information processed may include IP address, device and browser identifiers, page or ad interactions, approximate location and related technical data. Third-party vendors may use cookies based on previous visits to this site or other websites.

Before any advertising is activated, Tolvix will update this policy to identify the advertising services and vendors actually used, and will configure the consent and privacy controls required for the locations where ads are served. Visitors can review how Google uses information for advertising and manage available preferences through Google Ads Settings. Visitors can also use browser settings to block or clear cookies; this may affect some site features. Where available, Google provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Current advertising consent setup: Tolvix has not enabled an advertising CMP or an AdSense consent message because display advertising is not active. This is separate from the current analytics setup described above; there is currently no site-wide analytics consent banner or preference panel. Before enabling ads for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Tolvix will configure an appropriate consent solution for the applicable laws and Google publisher products. Google requires publishers serving personalised ads in these regions to use a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). See Google's publisher consent requirements and Google's CMP guidance.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use a tool that relies on an external service, adjust browser cookie controls, or use available provider-level privacy settings. Blocking some scripts may affect analytics, advertising or tool functionality. For privacy questions or requests relating to Tolvix, use the Contact page and avoid posting personal or confidential information in a public issue.
