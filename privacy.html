Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Overview

Tolvix is a collection of online utilities. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate, understand and protect the website.

Tool inputs

Many tools process text, numbers and selected files locally in your browser. Tolvix does not intentionally send those inputs to its own file-processing server when you use those tools. This includes the UTM Link Builder, Reading Time Calculator, Meta Tag Generator, Regex Tester, Markdown Table Generator and robots.txt Generator. Check each tool page for feature-specific details. Analytics may still record page usage and interaction events.

QR Code Generator

The QR tool uses the external service at api.qrserver.com. The text or URL you enter is sent to that service to create the QR image. The provider may process it under its own privacy practices. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into this feature.

Hosting and technical data

Tolvix is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

Google Analytics is used to measure website activity and may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on its configuration and your browser settings. The site also stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage under the key tolvix-theme; this preference keeps your selected appearance when you move between pages. Tolvix does not currently require an account or provide a site-owned account profile. Contact options are described on the Contact page.

Browser processing libraries

Some PDF features load the pdf-lib JavaScript library from the jsDelivr content delivery network so the PDF work can run in your browser. The provider may receive ordinary network request information when the library file is requested. The Word to PDF utility uses your browser's built-in print dialog; Tolvix does not upload the text to generate the print view. For tools that process content locally, Tolvix does not intentionally send that content to its own file-processing server. Check the individual tool page for its specific processing method and any external service it uses.

External links

We do not control third-party websites or services. Review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Information collected through website visits

When you open a page, the hosting and security infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps and security events. This information helps deliver the website, detect abuse and troubleshoot service problems. The handling and retention of infrastructure logs are subject to the relevant provider's terms and privacy documentation.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics measurement ID G-WH86KPZW58 to understand aggregate website activity, such as page views, traffic sources, device categories and interactions. Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on browser settings and the configuration in effect. Google processes analytics information under its own terms and policies. You can review Google's information at Google's Privacy Policy and learn about available controls in your browser or Google account.

Advertising and advertising cookies

Current status (20 November 2026): Tolvix has not enabled Google AdSense or other display advertising. No advertising cookies are intentionally set by Tolvix for ad delivery at this time. This statement describes the site's current status and will be revised before advertising is activated.

If Tolvix enables Google AdSense or another advertising service, that service and its participating vendors may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to deliver ads, limit how often an ad is shown, detect invalid activity, measure ad performance and, where permitted, personalise advertising. Depending on the visitor's location, browser settings and consent choices, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or limited. Google and other advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device and browser identifiers, page or ad interactions, approximate location and related technical data under their own privacy policies.

Before ads are enabled, Tolvix will update this policy to identify the advertising services actually used, explain relevant data practices and link to applicable vendor disclosures. Visitors can review how Google uses information for advertising and manage available preferences through Google Ads Settings. Browser controls may also allow cookies or local storage to be restricted or cleared, although this can affect website functionality.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If Tolvix begins serving ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, it will configure an appropriate consent solution before advertising is served in those regions. Google requires publishers serving personalised ads in these regions to use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The consent flow must explain relevant data uses and provide the choices required for the applicable purposes. See Google's publisher consent requirements and Google's CMP guidance.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use a tool that relies on an external service, adjust browser cookie controls, or use available provider-level privacy settings. Blocking some scripts may affect analytics, advertising or tool functionality. For privacy questions or requests relating to Tolvix, use the Contact page and avoid posting personal or confidential information in a public issue.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy when tools, analytics, hosting or advertising arrangements change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.
