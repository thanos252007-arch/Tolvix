Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Overview

Tolvix is a collection of browser-based utilities. We aim to collect as little information as reasonably necessary to operate the website.

Tool inputs

Most tools process text and numbers locally in your browser. Tolvix does not intentionally send those inputs to its own server when you use those tools.

QR Code Generator

The QR tool uses api.qrserver.com. The text or URL you enter is sent to that external service to create the image. The provider may process it under its own privacy practices. Do not enter confidential information.

AI Detector & Humanizer

The AI Detector & Humanizer uses local, rule-based text analysis and editing in your browser. Text entered into this tool is not sent to Tolvix's server or to an AI API by this tool. The style score is only a heuristic indicator, not an AI probability or proof of authorship. Review suggested edits because they may affect nuance.

Hosting

Tolvix is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Tolvix uses Google Analytics to understand website usage. Google may collect information such as page views, device/browser details and interaction data under its own policies. If advertising, accounts or forms are added, this policy should be updated to describe their data practices. Third-party services may use their own technologies under their policies.

External links

We do not control third-party websites. Review their policies before sharing information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the site changes. For privacy questions, use the Tolvix GitHub Issues page.
