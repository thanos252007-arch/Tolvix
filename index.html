Frequently asked questions

Are Tolvix tools free to use?

The tools listed on Tolvix are available to use without a paid subscription. Individual tools may have file-size, format or browser-compatibility limits; check the instructions on the tool page.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to use the tools currently listed on Tolvix.

Are my files uploaded to Tolvix?

Many supported tools process text and files in your browser. Some features rely on third-party services or libraries. Read the privacy note on the individual tool page and the Privacy Policy before using sensitive material.

Can I use Tolvix on a phone?

The website is designed to adapt to smaller screens. Some file formats or advanced operations may work differently depending on your mobile browser and device.
