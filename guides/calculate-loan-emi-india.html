How to Calculate Loan EMI in India: Formula and Example

An EMI estimate helps compare monthly payments, but it is not a lender quote or a substitute for reading the loan agreement.

← Back to Tolvix tools

Reviewed 6 November 2026. This practical guide explains the decisions and checks that help you use the related tool correctly.

Understand the task

For a standard reducing-balance loan, EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1). P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate as a decimal and n is the number of monthly instalments. Convert the annual rate consistently before calculating.

Important details to check

A longer tenure can lower the instalment but increase total interest. Actual lender schedules may include processing fees, insurance, prepayments, rate resets or other terms that a basic calculator does not model. Compare total repayment and fees, not only the EMI.

Recommended workflow

Enter principal, annual interest rate and tenure, calculate the estimate, then compare total interest and fees with the lender's official repayment schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a loan offer? No, it is an estimate. Can the actual EMI differ? Yes, lender terms, fees, rate changes and rounding can change the amount.
