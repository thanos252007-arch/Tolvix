How to Calculate SGPA for SPPU Students: Formula, Example and Calculator

Understand the credit-weighted SGPA formula, calculate a semester result step by step and use the grade points that apply to your own SPPU course and pattern.

← Back to Tolvix tools

To calculate Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA), multiply each course's credit value by its grade point, add those credit points, and divide by the total credits counted for the semester. This is a credit-weighted average, so a course with more credits has more influence on the result. Check the arithmetic with the Tolvix SGPA Calculator.

What is the SGPA formula used for SPPU?

SGPA = Σ (Course Credits × Course Grade Points) ÷ Σ Course Credits

For each course, calculate its credit points first. Add all credit points, then divide by the sum of credits included under your programme's rules. Credits are often shown as C and grade points as G in university documents.

SPPU has multiple programmes, admission patterns and course structures. The weighted-average formula is common, but the grade-point mapping, treatment of non-credit courses, failed courses and rounding must be taken from the rules for your own programme and pattern. Start with the official SPPU Examination Section marksheet information and your applicable syllabus or circular.

How to calculate your SPPU SGPA step by step

  1. Read your semester grade card and note each course's grade point and credit value.
  2. Check your programme rules to identify which courses count towards SGPA.
  3. Multiply each counted course's grade point by its credits.
  4. Add all credit-point results, then add the corresponding credits.
  5. Divide total credit points by total credits and apply your pattern's rounding rule.

To check your arithmetic, open the SGPA Calculator, choose the available grade-point scale, and enter each course's grade point and credits. The tool accepts values; it does not decide which SPPU rule applies to your course.

Does every SPPU course use the same grade-point table?

No. Do not automatically apply a grading table from another programme or admission pattern. Use the grade points printed on your grade card or the official programme-specific grading table. Do not convert raw marks into grade points using an unrelated online chart.

Common SGPA calculation mistakes

  • Taking a simple average of grade points instead of weighting by credits.
  • Using raw marks where the formula requires grade points.
  • Mixing grading rules from different SPPU patterns or programmes.
  • Counting non-credit courses contrary to the applicable regulations.
  • Rounding each course's contribution too early.

SGPA vs CGPA

SGPA represents performance for one semester. CGPA represents performance across the semesters or courses defined by the applicable rules. Both commonly use credit-weighted grade points, but the courses included and rules for repeats or improvements may differ. Do not assume multiplying SGPA by a fixed number gives an official percentage.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate SGPA if subjects have different credits?

Multiply each grade point by its own credits, add the products and divide by total credits. A higher-credit course carries more weight.

Can I calculate SPPU SGPA using marks?

You first need the grade point assigned to each course under your applicable SPPU grading scheme. Enter those grade points and credits into the formula.

How many decimal places should SGPA have?

Follow the rounding or truncation rule in your programme's official regulations and grade card.

Is the Tolvix SGPA result an official SPPU result?

No. It is an independent arithmetic tool. Your official result is the SGPA issued by SPPU or your authorised institution.
