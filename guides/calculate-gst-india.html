How to Calculate GST in India: Formula, Examples and Online Calculator

Learn the difference between a price before GST and a price that already includes GST, with simple examples in rupees.

← Back to Tolvix tools

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax applied to supplies of goods and services under India's GST system. When you see a price, it is important to know whether GST is yet to be added or is already included. The calculation method is different in those two situations.

This guide explains the arithmetic. It does not decide which GST rate or tax treatment applies to a particular product, service, seller or transaction. Confirm the applicable rate and current rules from official GST guidance or a qualified tax professional.

1. How to add GST to a price

When the amount you have is before GST, calculate the tax on that base amount and add it to the price.

  • GST amount = Base price × GST rate ÷ 100
  • Price including GST = Base price + GST amount

Example: adding 18% GST to ₹1,000

GST = ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180.

Total = ₹1,000 + ₹180 = ₹1,180.

2. How to remove GST from an inclusive price

If the total price already includes GST, do not calculate the tax by simply multiplying the total by the rate. Instead, divide by the rate factor to find the original taxable value.

  • Base price = Inclusive price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)
  • GST component = Inclusive price − Base price

Example: extracting 18% GST from ₹1,180

Base price = ₹1,180 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹1,000.

GST component = ₹1,180 − ₹1,000 = ₹180.

4. What about CGST, SGST and IGST?

For many intra-state taxable supplies, GST is collected as CGST and SGST/UTGST, while inter-state supplies are generally subject to IGST. The split and tax treatment depend on the transaction and applicable law. The Tolvix calculator shows the combined rate calculation; it does not determine place of supply or produce a tax invoice.

5. Common GST calculation mistakes

  • Applying tax twice: check whether the quoted price is tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive.
  • Using the wrong rate: rates depend on classification and current rules; do not assume one rate applies to every item.
  • Calculating inclusive tax incorrectly: use the inclusive-price formula rather than taking the rate percentage directly from the total.
  • Ignoring rounding: invoice-level rounding and accounting treatment may follow applicable rules. Check the figures required for your records.

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula to add GST?

Multiply the base price by the GST rate and divide by 100. Add the resulting tax amount to the base price.

How do I calculate GST included in a total?

Multiply the inclusive total by 100 and divide by 100 plus the GST rate. Subtract the resulting base price from the inclusive total to get the GST component.

Is 18% GST applicable to every product?

No. The applicable rate depends on the goods or services classification and current tax rules. Verify the correct rate for your specific transaction.

Does this calculator create a GST invoice?

No. It is a calculation aid only. It does not validate GSTIN details, classify supplies, determine place of supply or generate a compliant tax invoice.
