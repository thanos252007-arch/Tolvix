How to Calculate GST in India: Formula, Examples and Online Calculator

Calculate GST on a base price, extract tax from a GST-inclusive total and understand CGST, SGST and IGST in common examples.

← Back to Tolvix tools

To calculate Goods and Services Tax (GST), first check whether the amount is before tax or already includes tax. Adding GST and extracting GST use different formulas. This guide explains both with rupee examples and shows how to check the arithmetic using the Tolvix GST Calculator.

1. How to add GST to a price

GST amount = Base price × GST rate ÷ 100
Price including GST = Base price + GST amount

Example: add 18% GST to ₹1,000. GST = ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180. Total = ₹1,180.

2. How to remove GST from an inclusive price

Base price = Inclusive price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)
GST component = Inclusive price − Base price

Example: find GST included in ₹1,180 at 18%. Base = ₹1,180 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹1,000. GST component = ₹180. Do not calculate 18% of ₹1,180; that would use the wrong base.

4. How to calculate CGST and SGST from 18% GST

For an eligible intra-state supply where the confirmed combined rate is 18%, the tax is commonly split equally: 9% CGST and 9% SGST. On ₹1,000 taxable value, that example gives ₹90 CGST and ₹90 SGST. For an eligible inter-state supply, IGST may apply instead. Place-of-supply and transaction rules determine the correct treatment.

Common GST calculation mistakes

  • Applying the add-GST formula to an amount that already includes tax.
  • Assuming the same rate applies to every product or service.
  • Confusing CGST/SGST with IGST.
  • Rounding too early or ignoring transaction-specific adjustments.
  • Treating a calculator as a tool that confirms legal classification or invoice compliance.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 18% GST on ₹2,500?

₹2,500 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹450 GST. The total is ₹2,950 before other adjustments.

How do I find GST included in ₹2,950 at 18%?

Base = ₹2,950 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹2,500. GST included = ₹450.

Is 18% GST charged on every product?

No. Verify the applicable rate for the goods or service, classification and current rules.

Does the calculator create a GST invoice?

No. It performs arithmetic; it does not validate GSTINs, classify supplies, determine place of supply or create a compliant invoice.
