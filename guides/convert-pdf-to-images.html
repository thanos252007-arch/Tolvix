How to Convert PDF Pages to JPG or PNG Images

Learn when to export PDF pages as images, choose JPG or PNG and check resolution and page limits.

Converting PDF pages to images is useful when you need to place a page in a presentation, share a preview or upload an image to a service that does not accept PDFs. Each page becomes a separate image, so a long document can create many files.

Choose JPG or PNG

JPG is often suitable for photographic pages and smaller files. PNG is useful for diagrams, text-heavy pages and graphics where crisp edges matter. Output size depends on page dimensions, rendering scale and content.

Convert step by step

  1. Open the PDF to Image Converter.
  2. Select a PDF and wait for the rendering library to load.
  3. Choose the supported output format and resolution.
  4. Convert the required pages and save the images.
  5. Inspect small text, diagrams and page edges.

Resolution and limitations

Higher resolution can make small text clearer, but it also increases image dimensions, memory use and file size. Choose a resolution suitable for the final use. Converting a page to an image flattens selectable text and interactive PDF elements into pixels; links and form fields are not preserved as interactive objects.

Resolution, clarity and file size

When a PDF page is rendered as an image, the output resolution determines how much detail is captured. Higher resolution can make small text clearer when zoomed, but it also increases image dimensions and file size. Choose a resolution appropriate to the destination: a preview or chat attachment may not need the same detail as a page intended for close inspection or printing.

Check the exported pages

  • Confirm that the expected page range was converted.
  • Open a few images at full size and inspect small text, tables and diagrams.
  • Check page orientation and whether any annotations or layers are missing.
  • Use PNG for crisp text and diagrams; consider JPG for photographic pages when a smaller file is more important.

Rendering turns each page into pixels. The resulting JPG or PNG does not retain selectable text, links, form fields or the original PDF structure. Keep the source PDF if you may need to search, copy or edit its contents later.

FAQs

Will text remain selectable?

No. Text in a raster image is pixels, not selectable PDF text.

Why are the images large?

High resolution and complex page content create larger files. Try a lower resolution if it meets your needs.
