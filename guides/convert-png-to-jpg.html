How to Convert PNG to JPG and Handle Transparency

JPG is common for photographs, while PNG is useful for sharp graphics and transparency. Choose based on the image and its destination.

← Back to Tolvix tools

Reviewed 6 November 2026. This practical guide explains the decisions and checks that help you use the related tool correctly.

Understand the task

JPG uses lossy compression and does not support transparency. When converting a transparent PNG, the transparent areas must be filled with a solid background colour. White is common, but it may not suit the design where the image will appear.

Important details to check

Preview around fine edges, text and logos because the background fill can change the appearance. Keep the original PNG; repeated JPG saves can introduce further compression artifacts and cannot restore detail lost earlier.

Recommended workflow

Select the PNG, choose a background for transparent pixels, convert, preview the JPG and compare its size and quality before sharing.

Step-by-step checklist

  1. Read the instructions for the file, assignment or service you are working with.
  2. Keep an unchanged copy of important source material.
  3. Use the related tool and review the result instead of assuming every conversion is perfect.
  4. Check the output in the application or context where it will actually be used.

Frequently asked questions

Can JPG keep transparency? No. Will conversion restore detail? No; it changes format and may reduce file size but cannot add missing detail.
