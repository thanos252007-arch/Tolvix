Discount calculation explained

A percentage discount is calculated from the original price. For example, a 20% discount on ₹1,500 is ₹300, leaving a sale price of ₹1,200. The final price is the original price multiplied by one minus the discount rate expressed as a decimal.

Before comparing sale prices

  • Use the actual original price, not an unverified reference price.
  • Check whether shipping, taxes or additional fees are included separately.
  • For successive discounts, apply each discount to the updated price rather than simply adding the percentages.
  • Compare the final payable amount across sellers, not only the advertised discount.

This calculator estimates percentage savings. It does not verify a retailer's price history, coupon conditions or the final amount at checkout.
