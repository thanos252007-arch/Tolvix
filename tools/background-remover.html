Scanning your photo

Preparing your image

The AI is getting ready to work on your image.

Your selected image is processed on your device and is not uploaded to Tolvix. The AI model files (about 168 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face when first needed. First-time setup can take several minutes and uses substantial bandwidth and memory.

How to remove an image background

  1. Choose a clear JPG, PNG or WebP image with a visible foreground subject.
  2. Select Remove background and allow the browser to load the AI model if this is your first visit.
  3. Wait for processing to finish, then inspect the transparent preview.
  4. Download the result as a PNG to preserve transparency.

Best results

Photos with a distinct subject and a reasonably clear edge generally produce better cutouts. Fine hair, motion blur, transparent objects, shadows and subjects that blend into the background can be difficult for automated segmentation. Review the exported image before using it in a product listing or design.

Privacy, model and limitations

Image inference runs locally in the browser. The page downloads the open-source Transformers.js runtime and the selected ONNX model from their public hosting providers; your image is not sent to those providers by this tool. The first model download is large and can be slow on limited connections. Processing speed depends on the device and browser. This tool provides an automated cutout, not pixel-perfect manual masking.

Model: D30Social ORMBG browser-compatible ONNX export (Apache-2.0), based on the original ORMBG model. Runtime: Transformers.js. Review their licences and terms before redistribution or commercial deployment. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Frequently asked questions

Does this upload my photo?

No. The model runs in your browser. The runtime and model files are downloaded separately, but the selected photo is not uploaded by this page.

Why is the first run slow?

Your browser must download and initialise the AI model. Later runs may be faster because browser caching can reuse model files.

Why is the result not perfect?

Automated segmentation can miss fine hair, transparent materials, similar-colour edges or small foreground details. Check the PNG before publishing it.
