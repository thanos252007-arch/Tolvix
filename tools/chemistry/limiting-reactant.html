Limiting Reactant Calculator

Compare two reactants in a balanced reaction to identify the limiting reagent.

The limiting reactant has the smallest available mole amount divided by its balanced equation coefficient.

How to use this tool

Enter both reactant formulas, available masses in grams and balanced coefficients.
