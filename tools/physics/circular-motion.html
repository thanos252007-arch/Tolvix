Centripetal acceleration and force

Calculate the inward acceleration and net centripetal force required for an object moving around a circular path. Enter mass, speed and radius to connect circular motion with Newton's second law.

Formula and variables

Centripetal acceleration is ac = v²/r, and required inward net force is Fc = mv²/r. The direction is always toward the centre, even when speed is constant.

Worked example

A 1,000 kg car travelling at 10 m/s around a curve of radius 50 m requires 2 m/s² inward acceleration and 2,000 N of net centripetal force.

Assumptions and limitations

Centripetal force is not a separate type of force. It is the net inward force supplied by friction, tension, gravity or another interaction. The equations assume a circular path.
