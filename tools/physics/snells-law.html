Snell's law refraction angle

Calculate how a light ray bends when it passes between two transparent media. Enter refractive indices and an incidence angle to find the refracted angle or check whether total internal reflection occurs.

Formula and variables

Snell's law is n1 sin(θ1) = n2 sin(θ2). Angles are measured from the normal, not from the surface. Total internal reflection can occur when light travels from higher refractive index to lower refractive index above the critical angle.

Worked example

Light entering glass with n = 1.5 from air at 30° refracts to about 19.5° from the normal, assuming air has n ≈ 1.0.

Assumptions and limitations

Use angles measured from the normal and refractive indices for the relevant wavelength. Total internal reflection is only possible when light travels from a higher-index medium toward a lower-index medium.
