Simple pendulum period and frequency

Estimate the oscillation period and frequency of a simple pendulum. Enter its length and local gravitational acceleration to calculate the ideal small-angle motion often used in introductory mechanics.

Formula and variables

For small angular displacement, T = 2π√(L/g), and f = 1/T. L is pendulum length in metres and g is gravitational acceleration in m/s².

Worked example

A pendulum 1.0 m long near Earth's surface has an ideal period of about 2.01 seconds and frequency about 0.50 Hz.

Assumptions and limitations

The small-angle approximation works best for modest swing angles. Large swings, air resistance, pivot friction and a flexible string change the motion. Measure length from the pivot to the bob's centre of mass.
