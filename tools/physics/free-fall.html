Free fall speed and distance

Estimate the time, speed or vertical distance for an object falling from rest under gravity. This model is useful for introductory physics exercises where air resistance is ignored and gravitational acceleration is constant.

Formula and variables

For an object released from rest, distance fallen h = ½gt² and speed v = gt. Near Earth's surface, g is approximately 9.81 m/s². From a known height, time is t = √(2h/g).

Worked example

An object released from rest for 2 seconds falls about 19.62 m and reaches about 19.62 m/s in the ideal model.

Assumptions and limitations

This calculator assumes the object is dropped, not thrown, and that air resistance is negligible. Real objects can fall more slowly because drag matters. Check sign conventions when comparing upward and downward motion.

What formula is used?

Are these results exact in real life?

They are results from the stated idealised model. Real measurements can differ because of friction, drag, measurement uncertainty and other effects described above.
