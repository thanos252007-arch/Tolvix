Equivalent resistance in series and parallel

Find the equivalent resistance of a resistor network with components connected in series or parallel. This helps simplify basic circuit diagrams before calculating current and voltage.

Formula and variables

For series resistors, Req = R1 + R2 + …. For parallel resistors, 1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + …. For two parallel resistors, Req = R1R2/(R1 + R2).

Worked example

Two 6 Ω resistors in series give 12 Ω. The same two resistors in parallel give 3 Ω.

Assumptions and limitations

Use positive resistance values and ensure the network really is a simple series or parallel arrangement. More complex bridge circuits cannot be reduced by applying one formula to every resistor.
