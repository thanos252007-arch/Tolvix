SUVAT equations for constant-acceleration motion

Use this kinematics calculator for straight-line motion with constant acceleration. Enter the known initial velocity, acceleration and time to find final velocity and displacement. SUVAT equations connect displacement, initial velocity, final velocity, acceleration and time.

Formula and variables

The common equations are v = u + at and s = ut + ½at². Here u is initial velocity, v is final velocity, a is constant acceleration, t is elapsed time and s is displacement along the chosen axis.

Worked example

A vehicle starts at 4 m/s and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Its final velocity is 14 m/s and displacement is 45 m if acceleration remains constant.

Assumptions and limitations

Choose a positive direction before entering values. Negative acceleration does not always mean slowing down; it means acceleration opposite to the chosen positive direction. These equations are for one-dimensional constant-acceleration motion.

They are results from the stated idealised model. Real measurements can differ because of friction, drag, measurement uncertainty and other effects described above.
