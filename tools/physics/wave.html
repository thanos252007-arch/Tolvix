Wave speed, frequency and wavelength

Relate wave speed, frequency and wavelength for a periodic travelling wave. Use this calculator to solve a missing quantity when the other two are known, with consistent units.

Formula and variables

The wave equation is v = fλ, where v is wave speed, f is frequency in hertz and λ is wavelength in metres. Rearranging gives f = v/λ and λ = v/f.

Worked example

A wave travelling at 340 m/s with frequency 170 Hz has wavelength 2 m.

Assumptions and limitations

The equation applies to a travelling periodic wave in a specified medium. Wave speed depends on the medium and conditions; do not assume sound, water and electromagnetic waves all travel at the same speed.

No. The result follows the idealised model and assumptions explained above. Real measurements can differ because of experimental conditions and uncertainty.
