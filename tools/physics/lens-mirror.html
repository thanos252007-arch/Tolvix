Thin lens and mirror equation

Estimate image distance and magnification using the thin-lens equation. The relationship is useful for introductory ray diagrams involving converging or diverging lenses and spherical mirrors under the paraxial approximation.

Formula and variables

Using a consistent sign convention, 1/f = 1/do + 1/di. Magnification m = −di/do, where f is focal length, do is object distance and di is image distance.

Worked example

For a converging lens with f = 10 cm and an object 30 cm away, the image distance is 15 cm and magnification is −0.5 under the common real-is-positive convention.

Assumptions and limitations

Sign conventions differ between textbooks. Read the convention used in your course and interpret negative image distance or magnification accordingly. The thin-element approximation ignores lens thickness and strong off-axis aberrations.
