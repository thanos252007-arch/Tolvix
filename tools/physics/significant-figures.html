Significant figures and rounding

Round a measured number to a requested number of significant figures while retaining the scale of the original value. Significant figures communicate the precision of a reported measurement.

Formula and variables

For a nonzero value, count from the first nonzero digit and retain the requested number of significant digits. The next digit determines rounding. Scientific notation helps preserve trailing zeros that indicate precision.

Worked example

For example, 0.004567 rounded to three significant figures is 0.00457, or 4.57 × 10⁻³ in scientific notation.

Assumptions and limitations

Rounding rules can differ for exact halfway cases and formal laboratory reporting. Do not round intermediate values repeatedly; keep guard digits and round the final result to match the precision of the measurements.
