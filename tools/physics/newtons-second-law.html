Newton's second law: force, mass and acceleration

Find the net force needed to accelerate an object, or solve for mass or acceleration from the other two quantities. This calculator applies Newton's second law in a straight-line, constant-mass situation.

Formula and variables

Newton's second law is ΣF = ma, where ΣF is net external force in newtons, m is mass in kilograms and a is acceleration in metres per second squared. Use resultant force, not just one force acting on the object.

Worked example

A 5 kg trolley accelerating at 3 m/s² has a net force of 15 N. If several forces act, add them with their directions to find the net force.

Assumptions and limitations

Mass is not weight. Weight is a gravitational force measured in newtons, while mass is measured in kilograms. In two or three dimensions, apply the law separately to each component.
