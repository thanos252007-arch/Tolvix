Choose the right text case

Uppercase is useful for short labels or acronyms, while lowercase can standardise copied text. Title Case is often used for headings, but style guides differ on which short words should remain lowercase. Sentence case is generally easier to read in paragraphs and interface labels.

Review names and technical text

  • Check personal names, product names and abbreviations after conversion.
  • Do not assume every language follows English capitalisation rules.
  • Review code, passwords, file paths and case-sensitive identifiers before changing them.
  • Keep a copy of the original text if the exact capitalisation matters.

A case converter changes letter casing; it does not proofread spelling, grammar or brand-name conventions.
