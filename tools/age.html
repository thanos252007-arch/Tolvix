How an age calculation works

The calculator compares the date of birth with the selected current date and expresses the elapsed time as complete years, remaining months and remaining days. This is a calendar calculation, not simply the number of days divided by 365. Leap years and the different lengths of calendar months can affect the result.

Check the dates before using the result

  • Enter the date of birth in the correct day, month and year.
  • Check that the date is not in the future.
  • For an official form, follow the organisation's definition of age and its required reference date.

The result is a general date calculation. It does not establish legal eligibility, identity or entitlement; official rules may define age differently for a particular purpose.

Worked example: years, months and days

For a date of birth of 15 August 2004 and a reference date of 15 August 2026, the elapsed age is exactly 22 years. If the reference date is 11 November 2026, the calendar result is 22 years, 2 months and 27 days. The calculator uses calendar dates, so the result can differ from dividing the total days by 365.

Age calculator FAQs

Does this calculator include leap years?

Yes. It compares calendar dates, so leap days and month lengths are reflected in the years, months and days result.

Can I calculate age on a past date?

Use the reference date supported by the tool if available. For official applications, use the exact cutoff date stated by the organisation.

Why can two age calculators show different day counts?

They may use different reference dates, time zones or counting conventions. Compare the birth date and reference date entered in each tool.
