Limitations and privacy

PDF processing uses pdf-lib from jsDelivr. Large files may use substantial memory. Page extraction can remove document features such as outlines, forms or signatures; inspect the output.

Processing runs in your browser except for loading the PDF processing library from jsDelivr on the PDF Page Extractor page. Avoid entering secrets or confidential data into websites unless you have reviewed their privacy practices. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Load the PDF, enter the page numbers or ranges supported by the tool, process the selection and open the new PDF to verify its pages.

Is extracting pages the same as splitting a PDF?

Page extraction saves selected pages. Splitting usually means dividing a document into multiple files or ranges; use the PDF Split tool when you need separate sections.

Are my PDF contents uploaded?

The selected document is processed in your browser. The PDF.js library is loaded from a CDN; review the Privacy Policy for third-party requests.
