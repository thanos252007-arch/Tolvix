What makes a useful URL slug?

A slug is the readable part of a URL that identifies a page. Short, descriptive words separated by hyphens are generally easier for people to scan than long strings of punctuation or unrelated numbers. For example, a page titled “Beginner Guide to Image Compression” might use a concise slug such as image-compression-guide.

Review a slug before publishing

  • Keep the words relevant to the page topic.
  • Remove unnecessary filler words when the meaning remains clear.
  • Check that the slug is unique on your website.
  • If changing an existing public URL, set up an appropriate redirect and update internal links.

A slug generator creates a text suggestion; it does not check whether a URL is available on your site or configure redirects for you.
