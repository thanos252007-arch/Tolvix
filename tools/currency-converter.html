How to use the currency converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Select the source and destination currencies.
  3. Choose Convert to calculate the result using the latest rate data returned by the provider.
  4. Check the rate date and time before using the estimate.

How the conversion is calculated

The converter uses a common USD reference table. It converts the source amount into the reference currency, then calculates the destination amount using the destination currency's quoted rate. The result is rounded for display; the underlying calculation uses the available rate precision.

Important rate information

Exchange rates change over time. The displayed rate is an informational reference and may differ from the rate offered by a bank, money-transfer service, card issuer or currency exchange counter. Fees, spreads, taxes and settlement timing are not included. Do not use this estimate as a guaranteed quote for a transaction.

Data source and privacy

Rates are retrieved from the public ExchangeRate-API open access endpoint. Your amount and currency selections are used in the browser calculation; the request fetches a public USD-based rate table and does not need your personal information. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Frequently asked questions

Are the rates live?

The provider publishes periodically updated reference rates. They are not a real-time trading feed and may not reflect the exact rate available at the moment you exchange money.

Does the result include transfer fees?

No. Fees, provider markups, bank charges and taxes are not included.

Why can my bank show a different amount?

Banks and payment providers may use their own exchange rates, spreads, fees and settlement times.
