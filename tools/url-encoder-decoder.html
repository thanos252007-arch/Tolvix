Limitations and privacy

Frequently asked questions

When should I URL-encode text?

Encode data that will be placed inside a URL component, such as a query parameter value. Do not encode an entire URL blindly because separators such as ? and & have structural meaning.

What is the difference between encodeURI and encodeURIComponent?

They preserve different sets of reserved characters. Use component encoding for an individual parameter value, then construct the full URL with its separators.

Does encoding encrypt my text?

No. Percent encoding represents characters in a URL-safe form; it is not encryption or a privacy feature.
