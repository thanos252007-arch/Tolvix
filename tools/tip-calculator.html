Understand the bill split

The tip amount is calculated from the bill and the tip percentage. The total bill is the original bill plus the tip, and the per-person amount is the total divided by the number of people. For example, a ₹1,000 bill with a 10% tip produces a ₹100 tip and a ₹1,100 total; split equally between two people, that is ₹550 each.

Before paying

  • Check whether a service charge is already included in the bill.
  • Agree whether the tip is calculated before or after any discount or tax.
  • Use the correct number of people sharing the amount.
  • Confirm rounding and any uneven split with the group.

This is a simple arithmetic aid. It does not determine local tipping customs or replace the final amount shown by the restaurant.
