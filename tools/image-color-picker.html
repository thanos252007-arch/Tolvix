How to pick a colour from an image

  1. Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Select Load image and wait for the image to appear.
  3. Click the exact pixel you want to sample on the image.
  4. Read the sampled HEX and RGB values, then use Copy HEX if you need the hexadecimal value.

Example: Sampling a blue pixel may return a value such as #3366CC. The value represents the pixel selected, not an average of the surrounding area.

Limitations and privacy

Supports browser-decodable PNG, JPG and WebP files up to 25 MB and 25 megapixels. Very large images may take longer to load. Colour profiles, transparency, compression and browser rendering can affect the displayed appearance; the tool reports the pixel colour decoded by your browser.

The image is decoded and sampled in your browser; it is not uploaded to a Tolvix processing server. Avoid using sensitive images on shared devices. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for site-wide analytics and third-party requests.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a HEX colour from a photo?

Load the image, select the pixel you want to sample and copy the displayed HEX value. Nearby pixels may differ because of lighting, compression and anti-aliasing.

Why does the sampled colour look different on another screen?

Displays, colour profiles and browser rendering can affect appearance. HEX and RGB values describe the sampled digital colour, not a guaranteed physical print match.

Is my image uploaded?

Pixel sampling is performed in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for site-wide analytics and third-party requests.
