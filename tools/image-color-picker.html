Frequently asked questions

How do I get a HEX colour from a photo?

Load the image, select the pixel you want to sample and copy the displayed HEX value. Nearby pixels may differ because of lighting, compression and anti-aliasing.

Why does the sampled colour look different on another screen?

Displays, colour profiles and browser rendering can affect appearance. HEX and RGB values describe the sampled digital colour, not a guaranteed physical print match.

Is my image uploaded?

Pixel sampling is performed in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for site-wide analytics and third-party requests.
