How to use

Limitations and privacy

This tool prepares plain text for printing. It does not import DOCX files or preserve Word layouts, images, tables, headers or footers. Choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.

Processing runs in your browser except for loading the PDF processing library from jsDelivr on the PDF Page Extractor page. Avoid entering secrets or confidential data into websites unless you have reviewed their privacy practices. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save my text as a PDF?

Enter or paste your text, choose the available formatting options, then open your browser's print dialog and select Save as PDF.

Will the PDF look exactly the same on every device?

Margins, fonts and page breaks can vary by browser and print settings. Preview the pages and adjust scale or margins before saving.

Can I convert a DOCX file with this tool?

This tool formats text for printing; it is not a full Microsoft Word DOCX converter and does not preserve complex Word layouts.
