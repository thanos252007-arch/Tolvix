Free online word counter

Use this free word counter to check the length of essays, assignments, articles, emails, reports and other writing. Paste your text to see the word count and character count instantly. Tolvix keeps the tool simple: there are no signup steps, complicated settings or extra analysis panels.

What does the word counter show?

  • Words: the number of words in your text.
  • Characters: all characters, including spaces and line breaks.
  • Characters without spaces: the character count after whitespace is excluded.
  • Sentences: an estimate based on sentence-ending punctuation.
  • Paragraphs: non-empty text blocks separated by blank lines.
  • Reading time: an estimate using an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

How to count words online

  1. Open the word counter.
  2. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  3. Read the live word, character, sentence and paragraph counts above the buttons.
  4. Use Copy word count if you want to copy the total word count.

How does this word counter count words?

The counter uses your browser's built-in word-segmentation support when available, so it can recognise words in many writing systems, including accented Latin text and Indic scripts. Punctuation by itself is not counted as a word. Contractions are generally treated as one word, while hyphenated expressions may be split according to the browser's language rules. Different word counters can therefore show slightly different totals for the same text.

Who can use this word counter?

Students can check assignment and essay word limits. Writers and editors can measure article length. Professionals can check reports, emails and proposals. Anyone can use the character counter to check text length for forms or social posts.

Frequently asked questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. The Tolvix word counter is free to use and does not require an account.

Does the word count update while I type?

Yes. The counts update automatically as you type, paste or edit your text.

What is the difference between characters and characters without spaces?

Characters includes spaces and line breaks. Characters without spaces excludes whitespace such as spaces, tabs and line breaks.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is an estimate based on 200 words per minute. Your actual reading speed may be faster or slower.

Why can my word count differ from Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

Different tools may treat hyphenated words, contractions, numbers and some languages differently. For an assignment or submission, follow the counting rules given by your school, college or publisher.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The page adapts to mobile screens. Paste or type your text and the counts update automatically.
