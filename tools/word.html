Free online word counter and writing analyser

Tolvix is a free online word counter for essays, assignments, articles, emails, reports and social posts. Type or paste text to see a live word count, character count, sentence and paragraph totals, unique words, estimated page length and reading time. Set word goals, review keyword frequency, compare two drafts and export a report without creating an account.

What this word counter measures

  • Words and unique words: word-like groups and distinct word forms, based on your selected counting options.
  • Characters: Unicode code points, including spaces and line breaks; the no-spaces count excludes whitespace.
  • Sentences and paragraphs: sentence-ending punctuation estimates and non-empty blocks separated by blank lines.
  • Lines: text lines, including blank lines in non-empty text.
  • Writing statistics: average word and sentence length, longest word, estimated pages, reading time and speaking time.

Counting conventions differ between word processors and institutions. Hyphenation, contractions, numbers and language can affect results. Use the counting options to adjust common cases and follow the rules of the organisation receiving your work.

How to use the advanced word counter

  1. Type or paste your text. Statistics update automatically.
  2. Set a minimum, target or maximum word count.
  3. Adjust reading and speaking speeds to estimate time.
  4. Enter a keyword or phrase to check its count and density.
  5. Choose how hyphenated terms, contractions and numbers are counted.
  6. Compare two drafts, upload a plain-text TXT file, or export a TXT or CSV report.

Keyword density and readability estimates

The keyword checker reports exact case-insensitive phrase matches and the share of counted words occupied by the phrase. The frequent-word table lists repeated terms; it does not judge keyword quality or prescribe a target density. Flesch Reading Ease is an approximate English-language formula based on sentence length and estimated syllables. It is not a quality grade and may be inaccurate for names, abbreviations, code, mixed-language text or specialist writing.

Privacy and file support

Word counting, comparisons, TXT reading and report generation run locally in your browser. The TXT file is read by this page and is not sent to a Tolvix text-processing server. Normal website hosting and analytics may still collect limited technical or usage information; see the Privacy Policy. This version supports plain-text TXT files up to 2 MB, not DOCX or PDF imports.

Frequently asked questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. The listed analysis and export features are free to use without registration.

Does the counter update while I type?

Yes. Statistics, goal progress, time estimates, keyword analysis and readability update as you edit text or change counting options.

How are reading time and speaking time calculated?

They are estimates based on your selected words-per-minute settings. Actual time depends on pace, pauses and text complexity.

How many words are on a page?

Page count is estimated at 500 words per page. Real page length varies with font, margins, spacing, headings and layout.

How accurate is the readability score?

Flesch Reading Ease is designed for English. Syllables are estimated with a simple heuristic, so treat the score as a rough guide.

Can I upload a Word or PDF document?

This version accepts TXT files. Copy text from a Word or PDF document and paste it into the editor; DOCX and PDF import are not supported.

Why can my count differ from Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

Word processors may use different rules for hyphenated words, contractions, numbers, Unicode characters and languages. Review the counting options and follow your submission rules.

Word count tips for essays and assignments

Check whether your institution includes headings, quotations, citations, footnotes, tables or references. If your draft is 950 words and the target is 1,000, the difference is 50 words—but official instructions determine which sections count.
