How to use Reading Time Calculator

  1. Paste your article or enter its word count.
  2. Choose a reading speed in words per minute. 200–250 is a common general-purpose estimate, not a universal standard.
  3. Calculate the result and round it to a practical minute label.
  4. For technical or instructional content, consider adding time for examples, code and diagrams.

What to know

Reading time is an estimate, not a promise. A short news update, a technical tutorial and a text with many tables can take different amounts of time even when they have the same word count. Choose a speed that suits the audience and content.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal reading speed?

There is no single normal rate. Use 200–250 words per minute only as a rough starting point for general web copy, then adjust for your audience.

Does the calculator count images?

No. It estimates from text words only and cannot measure time spent studying images, code, tables or interactive examples.

Should I display exact seconds?

Usually not. Rounded minutes are easier to understand and avoid suggesting more precision than the estimate supports.
