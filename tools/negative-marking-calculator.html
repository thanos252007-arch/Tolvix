How negative marking is calculated

Final score = (Correct answers × marks per correct answer) − (Incorrect answers × penalty per incorrect answer).

Unattempted questions receive zero marks in this calculator. Enter the marking scheme specified in your exam instructions. Some exams use fractional penalties such as 0.25 or 0.33 marks per wrong answer.

Negative marking example

Suppose an exam awards 4 marks for each correct answer and deducts 1 mark for each incorrect answer. With 30 correct and 5 incorrect answers, the score is 120 − 5 = 115 marks. Unattempted questions score zero in this example.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter a fractional penalty such as 0.25?

Yes. Use the penalty value specified by your exam, including decimals where applicable.

Do unattempted questions reduce my score?

No. This calculator treats unattempted questions as zero marks. Check your exam rules in case they specify a different treatment.

Does accuracy include unattempted questions?

Accuracy is based on attempted answers, not all questions. Unattempted questions are reported separately.
