How to use

Preview CSV data as a table Select or enter your content, choose the available action and review the result before using it elsewhere.

Limitations and privacy

Supports quoted fields and escaped double quotes. The preview is limited to 5,000 rows and 100 columns for browser stability. It is a viewer, not a spreadsheet editor.

Processing runs in your browser except for loading the PDF processing library from jsDelivr on the PDF Page Extractor page. Avoid entering secrets or confidential data into websites unless you have reviewed their privacy practices. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Frequently asked questions

Why do some CSV values appear in the wrong column?

CSV fields containing commas, quotation marks or line breaks must be quoted correctly. Check the source file's delimiter and quoting rules if rows do not line up.

Can I use this to edit a spreadsheet?

This viewer is for inspecting parsed CSV data. It is not a full spreadsheet editor with formulas, formatting or multi-sheet support.

Is my CSV uploaded?

The CSV is parsed in your browser. Review the Privacy Policy for site-wide analytics and third-party requests.
