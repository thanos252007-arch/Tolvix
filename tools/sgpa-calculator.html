How SGPA is calculated

SGPA is generally calculated as the sum of each subject's grade point multiplied by its credit value, divided by the total credits for the semester.

SGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Enter the grade points and credits exactly as shown by your college or university. Grading rules and scales can differ, so this calculator does not convert marks into grade points automatically.

SGPA calculation example

Suppose three subjects have grade points 8, 9 and 7 with credits 4, 3 and 2. The weighted points are 32, 27 and 14, for a total of 73. Divide 73 by the total 9 credits to get an SGPA of approximately 8.11.

Frequently asked questions

How is SGPA different from percentage?

SGPA is a credit-weighted grade-point average for a semester. Percentage is a marks-based measure. Use only a conversion rule published by your university.

Can I calculate SGPA without credits?

Not reliably when subjects have different credit weights. Enter each subject's credit value so the result reflects the weighted calculation.

Does this tool convert marks into grade points?

No. Enter the grade points assigned by your institution. Grade boundaries can vary by programme and academic year.
