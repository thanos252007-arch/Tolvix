Important limitations

No reliable tool can determine the exact percentage of AI-written text from a paragraph. This free version uses simple, transparent text-pattern heuristics; it does not use a machine-learning model and cannot establish authorship. Its score must not be treated as proof.

The clarity improver uses a small set of rule-based edits. It is not an AI paraphraser, may make few or no changes, and cannot guarantee that meaning or nuance is perfectly preserved. It does not promise to bypass AI detectors or produce a “0% AI” result.

Privacy: text analysis and editing run in your browser. The text you paste is not sent to Tolvix or an AI API. Basic website analytics may still collect normal site-usage data.
