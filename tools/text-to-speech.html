How to convert text to speech

  1. Enter or paste up to 5,000 characters.
  2. Choose one of the voices installed or provided by your browser.
  3. Adjust speaking speed, pitch and volume if needed.
  4. Select Speak. Use Pause, Resume or Stop to control playback.
  5. To save an audio file, choose an AI English voice in the MP3 section and select Generate MP3.

Voice availability

The browser uses the speech synthesis voices provided by your operating system, browser or device. The available languages, accents and voice quality vary. Some devices load additional voices only after the system language or speech settings are configured.

Privacy and limitations

This tool uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API. Tolvix does not send the text to a Tolvix text-to-speech server. Depending on your device and selected voice, speech may be generated locally or handled by operating-system speech services. The browser voice playback and the downloadable AI voice generator are separate features. MP3 generation runs in your browser using an open model; text is not sent to a Tolvix speech server. The first model download is about 90 MB and may take time. Your device needs sufficient memory and a modern browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose another language?

Choose a voice whose language is listed in the voice selector. Only voices installed or exposed by your browser are available.

Can I download an MP3?

Yes. Use the Generate MP3 section and select an AI English voice. The generated audio is encoded as an MP3 file in your browser. This uses a separate set of AI voices, not the operating-system voice selected above.

Which English accents and voices are available?

The MP3 generator includes a selection of American and British English female and male character voices. Voice names are labels for the model voices, not custom voice cloning.

Does MP3 generation upload my text?

Speech synthesis runs locally in your browser after the model files have downloaded. The model files are fetched from the public model host; your entered text is not sent to a Tolvix TTS server.

Why is the voice list empty?

Some browsers populate voices asynchronously. Wait a moment, reopen the selector or check your operating system's speech settings. Use a browser that supports the Web Speech API.
